## 3. Rules found on the Judging & Awards Guide (edition not verified as 2026)
Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide
- "Complete all of the required fields on the Project Submission page." (fields not listed there)
- Submit "by navigating to the project tab on your team page, click edit, and then "submit"".
- Teams must "Respond to one of the official NASA Space Apps challenges." "Create Your Own Challenge" projects are not eligible for Global Judging.
- Team size: "The maximum number of team members allowed is six, and the minimum is one."
- Sources "listed in the References field of the project submission form."
- "Use English language on the project submission page and in any linked materials."
- Demo video: may speak another language only if "it MUST include English language subtitles." No length/format/size rules found. Character limits, pre-event work, AI use: not on this page.
