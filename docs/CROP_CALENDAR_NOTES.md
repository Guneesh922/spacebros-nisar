## 2026 Cyclone Arnab crop-damage reporting (post-event, news; figures differ between outlets, treat as preliminary)
4. Hans India, 30 Oct 2026: https://www.thehansindia.com/news/cities/amaravati/cyclone-leaves-farmers-staring-at-ruined-harvests-1137256

6. Daily Pioneer, 29 Oct 2026: https://dailypioneer.com/india/cyclone-arnab-leaves-7-dead-causes-rs-4600-crore-loss-in-andhra-pradesh-345-lakh-farmers-affected
