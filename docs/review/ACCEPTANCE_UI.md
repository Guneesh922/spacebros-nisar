## Bugs found

**B1. Arnab wording outside an Arnab chip (check 2).**
Repro: open `http://localhost:8765/app/dashboard-mockup/#tab=happened&mode=share&d=2026-11-09`, read the text. "Landfall day", "1 day before landfall" and "calm centre (eye) of Cyclone Arnab" appear under "Earlier updates" on the home page. "12 days after landfall" and the Murasu/river-peak caveat appear in the 9 Nov card. Source: `data/processed/findings/latest.json` (`sub`, `lead`, `caveats`, `updates`). Fix: gate these sentences on `event_chip`, or move them to `events.json` `story`.

**B2. Auto-drafts from alerts are marked "Radar passes linked" instead of alert_only (check 4).**
Repro: home page, "Events archive", "Tropical Cyclone MURASU-26" and "Flood in India". Source: `data/processed/events.json`, `auto_GDACS_1001346` and `auto_GDACS_1104248` have `status: "radar_linked"`, `status_text: "Radar passes linked"`, `reviewed_by_human: false`. Plan (WAVE4_PLAN.md, events-v1) says auto drafts stay `alert_only` ("Alert only, not yet seen by radar"), with linked passes shown separately.

**B3. Finding wording does not match the plan's fixed phrases (check 4).**
Repro: home page, "Events archive", "Cyclone Arnab", "Radar passes linked to this event". 28 Oct: "No mandal looks probably flooded in this radar picture." (plan: "Radar shows no clear change"). 9 Nov: "More land is probably flooded than on the last radar pass." (plan: "Radar shows likely new open water in N mandals"). Source: story/finding templates behind `renderStory()`.

**B4. Console errors in the main tab (check 8), unconfirmed.**
Repro: load the home page in the main tab and read console. Shows `a.sachet.map is not a function` (dash.js:744) x4 and `Cannot read properties of null (reading 'addEventListener')` (dash.js:1149). The current file does not match those line numbers and a fresh tab showed no errors. Re-check with a clean profile; if it reproduces, the alerts code path that reads `a.sachet` as an array needs fixing.

**B5. Two different "updated" times on one page (header vs footer).**
Repro: home page. Header reads "Data updated 14 Nov 2026, 11:30 IST" (dash.js:107 and 1410, from `manifest.generated_utc`). Pass footer reads "Page updated 14 Nov 2026, 20:36 IST" (findings `built_utc` 15:06:20Z). Alerts say "Checked 19:59 IST". Pick one source for the page-level time.

**B6. Contradiction in the "How radar sees water" panel.**
Repro: home page, "What radar cannot tell you", "Radar tells you the flooded area in square kilometres" (under "Radar cannot"). The same page shows "84 KM² of likely new open water". Source: `app/dashboard-mockup/js/findings.js:81`. Reword to "Radar cannot tell you the depth or the damage", or remove the claim.

**B7. Sea difference layer title and limits describe different dates (check 10).**
Repro: Data tab, Layers table. Row title "Sea roughness change (8 Nov minus calm 15 Oct)", dated 8 Nov 2026. Its Limits text says "27 Oct reads about 4 dB brighter in VV ... than 15 Oct, while 8 Nov differs from it by only about 0.1 dB". Make the title and date match the difference that is shown (STATUS.md names 27 Oct minus 15 Oct).
